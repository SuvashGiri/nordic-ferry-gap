# The Ferry Gap

Norway electrified its ferries. The rest of the Nordics are next.

**Live:** https://suvashgiri.github.io/nordic-ferry-gap/

An opportunity note: where the Nordic ferry electrification gap is, who is paying to close it (large, well-funded operators and agencies), and what that means for the small Nordic ship designers that supply them: consolidation helps only with reusable ferry classes and lifetime work.

Built from public sources: Statens vegvesen, tilnull.no, Trafikverket, Shippax, Baird Maritime, Corvus Energy, Siemens/Bellona. Country measures differ and are not strictly comparable. Sizing is an own, labelled estimate.

Independent opportunity note by Suvash Giri, built with an AI assistant. Not investment advice. Not affiliated with any company named.
